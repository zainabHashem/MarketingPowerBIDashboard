# Market Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **Power BI dashboard for market analysis and marketing intelligence**, designed to transform raw sales, pricing, and customer data into **actionable insights**.  
It supports revenue optimization, competitive benchmarking, and strategic decision-making within a **general consumer goods market**.

The dashboard reflects real-world business scenarios where teams analyze performance trends, regional dynamics, and competitor strategies to drive sustainable growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Evaluate sales performance by **region, product, and channel**
- Benchmark company pricing against **market and competitor averages**
- Track **market share** and competitor positioning
- Identify growth opportunities across **high-value segments and regions**
- Enable data-driven decisions for **pricing, targeting, and market expansion**

---

## 📊 Key KPIs
- **Total Revenue:** 3B (with regional breakdowns)
- **Total Units Sold:** ~3M (quarterly trends)
- **Average Unit Price:** 1.20K vs. **Market Average:** 1.22K
- **Revenue by Region:** Delta (20.9%), Giza (20.8%), Cairo (19.9%)
- **Competitor Market Share:** Balanced at ~23% per major player (X, Y, Z)
- **Customer Segments:** Enterprise accounts for 36% of customers and 50% of total volume

---

## 📁 Dataset Structure
The dataset consists of interconnected sheets to support comprehensive analysis:

### **Sales_Data**
- Date, Region (Delta, Giza, Cairo)
- Product (A, B, C), Customer, Channel (Direct, Retail, Wholesale)
- Units Sold, Unit Price, Revenue

### **Market_Prices**
- Average market prices by region, product, and quarter  
- Used for pricing trends and benchmarking analysis

### **Competitor_Analysis**
- Competitor prices, regions, and estimated market share (%)  
- Powers competitive comparisons and heatmaps

### **Customer_Segments**
- Customer details, regions, segments (Enterprise, Medium, Small)
- Annual volume and priority levels  
- Enables segmentation and key-account insights

---

## 🧠 Insights Generated
- **Regional Hotspots:** Delta and Giza contribute ~41% of total revenue (620M and 614M respectively) despite balanced unit sales (~510K each), indicating strong pricing leverage in these regions.
- **Pricing Dynamics:** Unit prices declined in Q3 (1.196K) amid competitive pressure (competitor avg: 1.198K vs. market: 1.224K), highlighting opportunities for targeted promotions to recover 3–5% market share.
- **Product Performance:** Product C leads revenue contribution (35%) but underperforms in Upper Egypt, suggesting inventory reallocation toward high-demand regions such as Delta.
- **Competitive Landscape:** Competitor X dominates Delta (25% share), while Competitor Z underperforms in Alexandria (<20%), presenting opportunities for region-specific counter-strategies.
- **Customer Focus:** The top 10 customers—primarily high-priority Enterprise accounts in Cairo—represent ~20% of annual volume; strengthening retention could drive up to 10% overall growth.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Interactive Filters & Slicers:** Region, product, channel, and competitor selection
- **Drill-Down Capabilities:** From quarterly to monthly views for granular trend analysis
- **Dynamic Visuals:**  
  - Line charts for pricing and revenue trends  
  - Bar and pie charts for regional and segment contributions  
  - Heatmaps for competitive and regional comparisons
- **User-Friendly Design:** Tooltips, annotations, and clear KPI highlights for quick decision support

## 🚀 Business Value
This dashboard enhances market analysis by:
- Delivering interactive insights that reduce decision-making time from days to minutes
- Supporting actionable strategies such as regional pricing optimization (potential +100M revenue uplift)
- Enabling stakeholders to proactively identify risks (e.g., Q3 downturns) and growth opportunities (e.g., Alexandria expansion) using data-backed recommendations
